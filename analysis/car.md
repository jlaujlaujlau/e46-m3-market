## The one real objection

~4,900 miles in ten years is excellent for the odometer and hard on seals, fluids and
bearing surfaces. "Never had rod bearings, sat for a decade" is the question that will
dominate a BaT comment section. Two credible answers:

1. Complete the VANOS service and get a bearing **inspection** on record before listing.
   Model says VANOS alone is worth ~+$7,800 — comfortably more than the job costs.
2. Present it as a preserved, unmolested original and let the buyer budget the work.

A full rod-bearing job is optional — the data shows no premium paid for it. Being able to
say the bearings were *inspected* with clearances documented removes the objection at a
fraction of the cost.
