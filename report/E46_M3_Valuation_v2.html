Two things I told you last week that were wrong

Cinnamon is +16.7%, not +35%. Still highly significant and still the single best thing about your car after the odometer — but roughly half what I quoted. A refit on the recent window alone still shows ~+31%, so a genuine re-rating is possible. I tested it: the interaction is not significant (p=0.19), and with only 13 Cinnamon cars before 2021 there isn't power to call it. The full-sample figure is the defensible one.

Slicktop is worth nothing measurable — −0.2%, p=0.96. I reported +14.8% and called it "a floor" that would rise once the data distinguished confirmed moonroofs from unknowns. It did the opposite: with 781 cars now positively confirmed as having moonroofs, the premium evaporated. The v1 figure was an artifact of comparing advertised slicktops against a contaminated everything-else group. Good news for you incidentally — your moonroof costs nothing.

The mileage curve, corrected

v1 forced a straight line through log-mileage. That overpredicted sub-25k cars by about 9% and underpredicted the 25–40k band by about 6% — and your car sits in that underpredicted band. A cubic spline flattens residuals to under ±2% everywhere and is worth about +$3,200 to your estimate.

What the re-scrape cost

The fixes were real — all five extraction bugs corrected, coverage up from 200 rows to 937, and I verified the three known-bad colours independently. Two things got worse, both unavoidable:

  • The accident penalty is gone. BaT renders its Carfax panel in client-side JavaScript, so a bulk scrape can't see it — the column is now empty on all 937 rows. v1 measured a −16.8% accident discount; that's no longer estimable. It doesn't affect your valuation (your own Carfax PDF is clean and independent of this dataset), but it's a real loss to the model. Never read the empty column as "no accidents" — it means unknown.
  • Mileage is coarser. Exact Carfax odometer readings came from the same widget, so 94% of mileages are now rounded to the nearest thousand, up from 87%. Minor, but it's the model's most important input.

Also worth knowing: 39 listings state no mileage anywhere and drop out of the fit; 109 sold cars have no interior colour and are carried as an explicit Unknown level rather than discarded.

Method

OLS on log sale price. n=651 BaT-sold non-modified E46 M3 coupes, 2014–2026, from the corrected 937-row file. Cubic spline (df=4) in log-mileage; year fixed effects; controls for transmission, verified Competition Package, paint, interior, disclosed rod-bearing / RACP / VANOS work, roof status and reserve. R²=0.807, 5-fold cross-validated median absolute error 12.6%. All headline coefficients confirmed against a Huber robust re-fit with negligible movement. Modified, supercharged, swapped and track cars excluded. Mechanical-work fields remain seller free-text and are weak signals. Auction results carry irreducible variance from bidder composition, photography, timing and reserve strategy — the range is the answer, not the point estimate.
